Privacy and runtime behavior
   No third-party CDN scripts, iframe games, analytics or external audio calls
   are added by the Playroom. The cat sprites are served from this site.
   Game progress, best score and companion preference are browser-local only.
   Clearing browser data resets them. Private/restricted storage may not save.
   Sound is opt-in per component session. No global keystrokes are collected.
   Keyboard handlers operate only while the corresponding game is focused.
   Confetti is lazy-loaded and honors the system reduced-motion setting.
